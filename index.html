Squish — Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 September 2026

Squish is a game. There is no account, no sign-in, and nothing asks who you are. It never touches your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone. Everything the app collects, or sends anywhere, is on this page.

What stays on your phone

Your scores, coins, gems, unlocked jars and themes, ability charges and settings live in a single file on your device. That file is never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes it, and there is no copy anywhere else.

What leaves your phone

Your player name, score and country → dreamlo.com

The leaderboard. Sent automatically at the end of a Classic run; the other modes are never submitted. The name is one you chose — it does not have to be your real one, we do not verify it, and you can change it at any time on the Profile screen.

Your IP address → ip-api.com

To work out which flag to show beside you on the leaderboard — once, ever, and only if your phone's own region setting cannot be read. The only thing that comes back is a two-letter country code. We do not store the IP address.

Advertising identifiers, the ads you were shown, crash and performance data → Google AdMob

To show ads and to keep from repeating them. What Google does with it is governed by Google's own policy.

Purchases → Apple

Every in-app purchase is handled by Apple. Your card details and your Apple Account never reach us — we are told only that a purchase completed.

Ads, and what we do not do

Squish does not track you. Every ad request the game makes asks Google for a non-personalised ad: one chosen from the context it is shown in, not from anything you have done in other apps. The game never asks for permission to track you, because it never does — so iOS shows you no tracking prompt, and the advertising identifier that would make cross-app tracking possible is never used.

Nothing is sold or handed to a data broker. Nothing is combined with data about you from anywhere else.

Your choices

  • No ads at all. The Remove Ads purchase turns off the banner and the ads between runs, permanently.
  • Change your leaderboard name. Profile screen, any time. New runs go up under the new name.
  • Have your leaderboard entry removed. Write to the address below with the player name and it is deleted.
  • Remove everything. Deleting the app removes every trace held on your device.

Children

Squish is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.
